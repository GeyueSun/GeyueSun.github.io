  *with S. B. Nowak and T. Saadi Sedik* &nbsp;·&nbsp; **IMF Working Paper (forthcoming)**
  - Stablecoin transfers exceeded **US$7 trillion in 2025**. Using bilateral estimates covering **188 economies and 36,000+ corridors**, examines the geography and drivers of stablecoin flows in Asia and the Pacific, which accounts for about one-third of estimated global stablecoin flows.
  - Cross-border flows rise when Bitcoin returns fall and severe crypto selloffs shift activity toward stablecoins — a flight to safety *within* the crypto ecosystem — but flows decline as global risk aversion and US policy rates increase.
  - Flows are larger in economies with **tighter capital controls and weaker political and legal institutions**, while inflation, depreciation, remittances, and bilateral trade show little systematic association.
