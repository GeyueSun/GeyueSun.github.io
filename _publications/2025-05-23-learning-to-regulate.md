  *with X. Liu, T. Williams, and R. Samaniego* &nbsp;·&nbsp; ***The Journal of Finance and Data Science***
  - Constructed a novel event-level dataset of **5,198 capital control measures** across 196 countries (1999–2023) from IMF AREAER reports, using a two-stage **GPT-4.1** framework that extracts 27 policy attributes.
  - Fine-tuned **Llama 3.1-8B (CCM-Llama)** for automated classification, achieving 90.09% accuracy in policy categories and 99.55% in status prediction.
  - An event-study application to China, Australia, and the U.S. finds that inward capital controls significantly reduce fund inflows within one month, with stronger effects from restrictive than liberalizing measures.
