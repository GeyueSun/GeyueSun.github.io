  **Job Market Paper**
  - Used **large language models** to classify SEC Forms 10-K, 8-K, and 20-F covering **6,551 U.S. domestic and foreign private issuers**, distinguishing crypto *mentions* from firms' actual crypto and stablecoin activities.
  - In **7.05 million firm-day observations**, Bitcoin–equity return sensitivity nearly triples from general crypto disclosures (0.045) to core activities (0.128), while mention-only firms are not sensitive at all; sensitivity is strongest for proprietary holders, smaller firms, and finance-industry firms (0.171).
  - Bitcoin returns also affect corporate operating income and asset growth; as more industry peers engage in crypto, Bitcoin reprices non-exposed business-services firms more strongly and non-exposed banks less. Stablecoin activity is mostly reserve holding and service provision rather than payment settlement.
