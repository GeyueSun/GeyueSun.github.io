  **IMF Working Paper (forthcoming)** &nbsp;·&nbsp; *with S. B. Nowak and T. Saadi Sedik* &nbsp;·&nbsp; work conducted during my **Fund Internship in the IMF Asia and Pacific Department**

  - Framed a regional policy question on what drives stablecoin flows and built data pipelines turning **6.25M raw USDT/USDC transfer records** into a country-month panel merged with IMF and BIS data, covering 188 economies and 36,000+ corridors.

  - Estimated panel, DiD, and gravity models: cross-border flows rise when Bitcoin returns fall — a flight to safety *within* the crypto ecosystem — but decline as global risk aversion and US policy rates increase; flows are larger in economies with **tighter capital controls and weaker institutions**.

  - Presented at an IMF APD seminar and the IMF MCM AI Community Seminar; results are cited in Vietnam Policy Notes.
