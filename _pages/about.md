Hi! I am Grace, a Ph.D. candidate in the [Department of Economics](https://economics.columbian.gwu.edu/) at [George Washington University](https://www.gwu.edu/) (Ph.D. expected May 2027), specializing in **International Macro-Finance and Digital Finance**, with secondary interests in **AI/ML in Economic Measurement and Asset Pricing**. **I am on the 2026–2027 economics job market.**

My research builds large-scale, AI- and text-based datasets to study capital flows, capital controls, crypto assets, stablecoins, and financial-stability risks. My job market paper, [**Not Just Talk: Crypto Activity, Equity Repricing, and Macro-Financial Transmission**](/job-market-paper/), uses large language models to classify SEC filings of 6,551 U.S. domestic and foreign issuers and shows that Bitcoin-related equity repricing is concentrated in firms with substantive crypto activities rather than mere crypto mentions. My work has appeared as an [NBER working paper](https://www.nber.org/papers/w34475) and a forthcoming IMF working paper, and I was a Fund intern in the IMF Asia and Pacific Department in summer 2026.

I am very fortunate to be advised by [Prof. Tomas Williams](https://tomas-williams.com/) at The George Washington University. Previously, I was mentored by [Prof. Guixia Guo](https://iie.uibe.edu.cn/english/faculty/60682.htm) during my Master's Degree at the [University of International Business and Economics (UIBE)](https://english.uibe.edu.cn/).

You can find my CV here: [Grace Sun's Curriculum Vitae](../files/CV_Geyue_Sun.pdf).
